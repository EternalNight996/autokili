OpenKill app icon (craft-anything.svg and every file derived from it in this directory:
craft-anything-1024.png, craft-anything.icns, craft-anything.ico and hicolor/).

Original artwork for OpenKill: pop-art crossed swords. It replaces the ArtCraft "raven"
icon, which the upstream assets require forks and modified versions to replace with their
own icon.
